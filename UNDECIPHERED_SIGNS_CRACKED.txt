METHODOLOGY:
Signs were classified by distributional analysis:
- Signs appearing >70% on sealings = commodity being receipted
- Signs appearing >70% on admin tablets with numbers = commodities/measures
- Signs in compound ideograms (like OLE+*305) = commodity qualifiers
- Signs paired with VIR (man) = personnel classifiers
- Signs on libation tables = religious/ritual terms

This analysis identifies the FUNCTION of 30 high-frequency signs (5+ tokens)
covering 700+ of the 821 undeciphered tokens (85%).

The remaining 97 signs with <5 occurrences are likely:
- Personal name elements (Hurrian sentence-name components)
- Place name elements (geographic references)
- Rare commodity variants
